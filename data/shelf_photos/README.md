# Shelf Photos Guide

This directory holds shelf photos collected from marts for produce freshness evaluation.

## Photo Guidelines
1. **Lighting & Angle**: Take well-lit photos facing produce bins directly without strong glare or deep shadows.
2. **Resolution**: Standard smartphone camera resolution (minimum 1080p).
3. **Framing**: Keep individual produce batches in frame with clear boundaries. Avoid extreme close-ups or blurry motion.
4. **Produce types**: Focus on high-turnover produce prone to rapid spoilage (e.g. mangoes, bananas, tomatoes, leafy greens).

## Annotation
Record batch metadata in `labels.csv` using the predefined header format. Real photos should be placed in this folder and referenced by filename.
