## Brand & Style

This design system defines a high-precision, AI-native grocery supply chain and autonomous negotiation console tailored for high-density retail markets. It bridges the gap between tactical operational efficiency and executive-level clarity. The visual language balances the utility of high-performance developer tools with the freshness, urgency, and tactile vitality of perishable food logistics.

The aesthetic fuses **Precision Functionalism** with **Modern Food-Tech Vitality**:
- **Tension & Polish**: Razor-sharp border definitions, balanced tabular density, and neutral slate architecture paired with organic, energetic botanical emerald and sunlit citrus accents.
- **Intelligent Agency**: AI voice orchestration and live autonomous deal negotiation are rendered through rich indigo accents, real-time waveform oscillations, and dynamic telemetry badges.
- **Atmospheric Clarity**: High-key luminous environments built on crisp cool slates, preventing cognitive fatigue in high-stress retail inventory and perishable salvage workflows.

## Colors

The palette is engineered for high data density, clear financial accountability, and low-latency cognitive processing under harsh lighting conditions.

### Architectural Neutrals
- **Canvas Base (`#F8FAFC`)**: Ultra-clean cool slate substrate providing soft contrast against pure white cards.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure white reserved for actionable cards, inspection tables, and floating telemetry panels.
- **Structural Borders (`#E2E8F0`)**: Crisp 1px structural boundaries defining the layout grid.
- **Muted Borders (`#F1F5F9`)**: Subtle sub-element dividers and internal table horizontal rules.
- **Primary Text (`#0F172A`)**: Deep ink slate for high legibility across dense metrics and Urdu/English labels.
- **Secondary Text (`#334155`)**: Mid slate for contextual details, unit specs, and status descriptors.
- **Tertiary / Caption Text (`#64748B`)**: Cool stone for metadata, timestamps, and inactive audio states.

### Semantic & Domain Accents
- **Primary Emerald (`#059669`) & Vivid Mint (`#10B981`)**: Represents positive inventory turnover, rescued margin, optimal batch vitality, and finalized deals.
- **Autonomous Intelligence Indigo (`#4F46E5`)**: Signals active AI agents, real-time voice call synthesis, negotiation heuristics, and neural pipeline activity.
- **Citrus Gold (`#F59E0B`) & Warning Amber (`#D97706`)**: Denotes imminent shelf-life expiry, dynamic markdown windows, and counter-offer pending states.
- **Critical Rose (`#E11D48`)**: High-priority alert state for severe loss risk, rejected thresholds, and failed line negotiations.

## Typography

Typography establishes an editorial-grade split between executive dashboard clarity, operational instruction, and financial telemetry:

- **Display & Section Headers**: Rendered in **Plus Jakarta Sans** with negative tracking to convey structural authority and clean modernism.
- **Interface & Operational Text**: Rendered in **Inter** to maximize readability across high-density tables, multi-turn AI negotiation transcript logs, and batch status tags.
- **Financial Figures, Units & Timers**: Rendered in **JetBrains Mono** with tabular numerals (`tnum`) enabled by default. Pakistani Rupee notations (`Rs.`), batch quantities (`kg`, `crates`, `dozen`), dynamic markdown countdown timers, and autonomous model confidence ratings must strictly use monospace tokens to prevent visual jitter during live updates.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a high-density, multi-panel structural model optimized for horizontal operational monitors and responsive field devices.

### Breakpoints & Layout Architecture
- **Desktop (1280px+)**: 12-column responsive layout with fixed sidebar navigation (260px) and a persistent or expandable side-rail (380px) for Live AI Voice Negotiation Telemetry.
- **Tablet / Small Display (768px - 1279px)**: 8-column layout. Negotiation inspector collapses into a sliding bottom drawer or modal sheet to preserve core table visibility.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column single-stack layout. Bottom dock holds rapid voice trigger actions and batch survival status feeds.

### Spacing Rhythm
Interior element spacing adheres strictly to a baseline 4px scale. Data table cells, metric strips, and log streams utilize tighter paddings (`space-sm`, `space-md`) to ensure critical batch quantities remain visible above the fold without requiring excessive scrolling.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses crisp hairline borders paired with ambient micro-shadows, creating structural separation without visually muddying the interface:

- **Level 0 (Base Substrate)**: Canvas background (`#F8FAFC`). Purely structural, flat, and non-reflective.
- **Level 1 (Cards & Data Panels)**: Pure white background (`#FFFFFF`) framed with a 1px solid stroke (`#E2E8F0`). Elevation is reinforced by an ultra-diffused micro-shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Flyouts & Floating HUDs)**: Surface white with an AI Indigo edge highlight (`rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.12)`) and elevation shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Dialogues & Emergency Overrides)**: Backdropped by a tinted frosted scrim (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.40)` with `backdrop-blur-md`). Shadow: `0 20px 32px -8px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.16)`.

## Shapes

The system implements balanced geometric roundedness (`0.5rem` / `8px` default radius for standard controls) to bridge modern desktop software precision with welcoming consumer-tech accessibility.

- **Primary Cards & Modals**: `12px` to `16px` outer corner radius to softly demarcate complex panels.
- **Buttons, Field Inputs & Selectors**: Standard `8px` (`rounded-md`) to provide confident clickable affordance.
- **Telemetry Badges, Audio Track Indicators & Status Pills**: Full rounded pill geometry (`9999px`) to emphasize real-time, non-editable status indicators.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Operational Button**: Emerald gradient fill (`#059669` to `#047857`), solid white text, subtle top inner-bevel shadow (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`), crisp border (`#047857`). Used for "Accept Rescue Deal" or "Initiate Mart Dispatch".
- **AI Action / Voice Trigger Button**: Rich Indigo fill (`#4F46E5`), pulsing indigo halo highlight on active voice generation, white text.
- **Secondary / Ghost Button**: White surface with 1px border (`#E2E8F0`), hover state elevates background to `#F8FAFC`, slate text (`#0F172A`).
- **Destructive Button**: Subtle rose surface (`#FFF1F2`), rose border (`#FECDD3`), red text (`#E11D48`), hover transitions to solid crimson.

### Badges & Status Chips
- **Batch Expiry Chip**: Citrus Amber background (`#FEF3C7`), border (`#FDE68A`), text (`#B45309`), formatted with an urgency icon and time remaining (`04h 12m`).
- **AI Status Badge**: Pill shape. Indigo tint (`#EEF2FF`), border (`#C7D2FE`), text (`#4338CA`), with an active 6px pulsing green dot representing automated voice negotiation in progress.
- **Financial Arbitrage Tag**: Mint tint (`#ECFDF5`), border (`#A7F3D0`), text (`#065F46`), showcasing margin recovered (`+Rs. 4,250 Saved`).

### Form Controls & Inputs
- **Numeric & Pricing Inputs**: JetBrains Mono font face, integrated left-aligned currency affix (`Rs.`), right-aligned unit indicator (`/kg`). Border `#CBD5E1`, focusing with an emerald ring (`ring-2 ring-emerald-500/20 border-emerald-600`).
- **Sliders & Step Inputs**: Used for live judge simulation toggles (e.g., "Urgency Modifier", "Wholesale Counter-Offer Aggressiveness"). Track uses cool slate `#E2E8F0`, active fill in botanical emerald `#059669`.

### Produce Card & Inventory Matrix
- Grid cards display perishable item name in Plus Jakarta Sans bold, high-resolution visual thumbnail, shelf-life progress meter (gradient color transitions from emerald -> amber -> rose), and instant unit economics (`Rs. 180 -> Rs. 95/kg`).

### Domain Specific: Live Voice Call Visualizer & Simulation Rail
- **Audio Waveform Canvas**: High-frequency oscillating sound bars rendered in `#4F46E5` transitioning to `#10B981` during conversational agreement. Inactive bars rest at `#CBD5E1`.
- **Live Urdu/English Transcript Bubble**: Crisp cards with speaker metadata pill (`Agent: LastCall AI` vs `Buyer: Badami Bagh Mart`), auto-scrolling with synchronized confidence intervals.
- **Judge Interactive Override Toolbar**: Floating bottom-right dock framed with a glass stroke (`backdrop-blur-md bg-white/90 border border-slate-200`) providing simulation reset, artificial buyer refusal injection, and live profit-loss arbitrage calculation.
